# Frame Brief: PDF extraction and golden acceptance

> Framing during Phase 4, 2026-10-03. Read-only local investigation;
> no provider calls, production edits, golden changes or acceptance relaxation.

## Reported Observation

Modelowe wyniki nie są zgodne z zatwierdzonymi źródłami.

The latest retained evaluation reports summer with one retained candidate,
four invalid candidates and three missing golden recipes; pasta has five
retained candidates, one invalid candidate and field-comparison failures.
These are observations about the pipeline output, not a diagnosis of model capability.

- **Prior assistant direction:** "iterować konfigurację, aż raport będzie zielony".

- **Pre-dispatch narrowing:** "oba - braki w summer i bledne pola w pasta";
  "golden ma byc jedynym kryterium akceptacji"; "narazie tylko na nich".
- The golden content remains authoritative. Only the existing approved whitespace
  and line-wrap normalization applies; semantic similarity is not a substitute for acceptance.

## Dimension Map

1. **Reader-to-provider input:** layout and category context may disappear between
   the verified reader and the actual request.
2. **Prompt, validation and reconciliation:** candidates may be omitted or rejected;
   structural completeness may be confused with exact source accuracy.
3. **Golden comparison and evidence:** aggregate labels may misdescribe differences;
   saved artifacts may be insufficient to establish why an attempt failed.

## Narrowing Signals

- Both ebooks remain required; neither may be dropped from the acceptance set.
- Golden is the sole accuracy criterion. A corrected description of an error
  does not turn a nonmatching field into an accepted result.
- All pasta final meal categories and source pages are correct in the latest
  retained output. Its combined category/page failures come from sourceCategory text.
- All pasta instructions, servings and footnotes match. The combined
  variants/steps/notes failures come from ingredient-group labels.
- All 67 pasta ingredient names match. All 67 quantity/unit pairs differ:
  runtime quantity contains the golden quantity and unit within a longer string,
  while runtime unit is null. This proves representation mismatch, not necessarily
  incorrect numeric amounts. Eleven sourceText fields differ across four recipes.
- All five pasta ingredient-group labels should be null under the approved
  unlabelled-group convention; runtime supplies a label instead.
- The surviving summer candidate still differs in ingredients, group labels,
  instructions and footnotes. Being structurally accepted is not golden accuracy.
- Invalid provenance is a broad code: absent raw candidates prevent proving
  whether a context-page anchor, nonexistent item or another condition caused it.
- There are no reconciliation conflicts in the latest two reports.

## Cross-System Convention

This repository already distinguishes reader validity, schema completeness and
source accuracy (`research.md`, Architecture Insights; `plan.md`, Phase 4).
Its earlier layout review explicitly requires positional/column information
through the backend (`context/foundation/pdf-import-review.md`, Accepted MVP flow).
The input loss therefore deviates from an existing requirement.

Independent counterchecks were local and in memory: geometry-only perturbation
left provider input unchanged; golden-versus-itself comparison passed; a separate
field comparison confirmed the pasta findings. Golden identity was checked against
the pinned manifest by the evaluation investigation. No private content was added here.
Projecting golden recipes into the runtime object shape also produces zero errors:
there is no evidence of an incompatible golden schema. An in-memory diagnostic
that isolates sourceCategory, label and quantity/unit differences leaves exactly
four pasta ingredient-text failures; it changes no files and is not an accepted result.

## Reframed Problem Statement

> The problem is an extraction-and-evaluation pipeline that discards available
> layout information and lacks sufficiently precise, reproducible failure evidence,
> while its retained results still fail the approved golden contract.

The initial focus on prompt tuning alone is not supported. Both the extraction
input and the interpretation of its output have demonstrated gaps. Those gaps
must be separated from actual content differences before judging model feasibility.
This does not establish that correcting the gaps will achieve full accuracy.

## Confidence

**HIGH** for the observed implementation gaps and corrected field-level findings.
**MEDIUM** for how much each gap explains summer's losses; rejected payloads and
earlier attempt outputs were not retained. Model capability remains undetermined.

## What Changes for /10x-plan

The remaining Phase 4 work must address fidelity of source information and
trustworthiness of golden acceptance evidence, alongside actual extraction accuracy.
The plan must retain both fixtures, exact golden acceptance and existing budget,
privacy and time constraints; no model/provider change follows from this brief.
Phase 4.4 remains pending. No commit or later-phase completion is implied.
